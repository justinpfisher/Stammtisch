03 / The tradition

A life remembered.
A story shared.

At the next Stammtisch following a passing, the member presents a Celebration of Life speech about that person’s life and contribution to the world.

How the points work

100−age at passing=points
  • A passing at age 100 earns 10 points.
  • At age 101 or older, 100 minus age gives a penalty: −1 at 101, −2 at 102, and so on.
  • The first and last selections carry double points in the spreadsheet.

“Awarded” points contribute to the standings. For other selections, “Value” shows the points at the age shown. Ages and values use the register’s update date.

Notes on scoring +

Recorded awards and agreed exceptions take precedence over estimates. Kevin Keegan’s 25 points were apportioned 12 to Matt and 13 to Ken. Dolly Parton’s 20 points were already included in Jamie’s total.

Where an award has not yet been recorded, the age-at-death rules determine the provisional figure.

04 / The pool

Official rules.

Jerome’s original wording

CELEBRATION OF LIFE POOL

We each have a list of 50 celebrities.

When one of our celebrities dies, we win points.

Most points each year wins.

POINTS+

The points equal 100 minus age at death.

If your celebrity dies at 100, you win 10 points.

If your celebrity dies at 101 or older, you lose one point for every year over 100.

DIAMONDS+

The first and last celebrities on your list are your Diamonds.

#1 is your Blue Diamond.

#50 is your Brown Diamond.

You get double points when a Diamond dies (or lose double points, if they’re over 100.)

STEALS & PROTECTIONS+

You can win Steals and Protections. These get used at the annual draft.

STEAL

Take a celebrity from someone else’s list and add them to yours.

  • You can’t steal a Diamond.

  • A celebrity can’t be stolen twice in the same year.

PROTECTION

Make a celebrity on your list unstealable.

  • Protections last until the celebrity dies.

  • Protections can’t be transferred or cancelled.

There’s three ways to win Steals and Protections.

  1. Diamonds

    When your Blue Diamond dies, you win a Steal.

    When your Brown Diamond dies, you win a Protection.

  2. Oldest / Youngest Death

    The oldest death of the year wins a Steal.

    The youngest death of the year wins a Protection.

  3. Devli’s Share

    The player with the fewest points chooses either a Steal or Protection.

BIRTHDAY BUFFET+

If a celebrity from anyone’s list dies on your birthday, you win the points.

  • Birthday Buffet is based on when the death is reported in our group chat (not date of death.)

  • A player cannot withhold information of a death to benefit.

  • Diamonds and Protections are excluded from Birthday Buffet.

BIRTHDAYS+

WINNING+

The Celebration of Life Pool lasts from Renewal Stammtisch to Renewal Stammtisch.

The winner is the one with the most points on that day, at an agreed-upon time.

The prize is a $100 gift, paid for and chosen by the other five.

This prize is awarded at the following Stammtisch, ideally the cottage. That’s also when we have the Button Ceremony.

ANNUAL DRAFT+

ORDER OF BUSINESS

  1. WINNER DECLARED

  2. PROTECTIONS

  3. STEALS

  4. DEVIL’S SHARE

  5. JETTISONS

  6. DRAFT

  7. CORONATION OF DIAMONDS

1. WINNER DECLARED

2. PROTECTIONS

Anyone who won a Protection that year chooses a celebrity on their list to protect from Steals, effective immediately.

3. STEALS

Anyone who won a Steal that year chooses a celebrity from someone else’s list and adds them to their own.

  • Steals happen in the order the Steals were won.

  • A celebrity can’t be stolen twice in the same year.

4. DEVIL’S SHARE

The person with the fewest points chooses either a Steal or Protection, and uses it immediately.

5. JETTISONS

If someone didn’t have five deaths that year, they can remove celebrities from their list to create up to five spaces.

Once all players have five spots, or chose not to jettison, jettisons are complete.

You cannot jettison…

  • Diamonds

  • Protections

  • Celebrities over 100

If you jettison a 100-year-old and they die before turning 101, you lose ten points.

6. DRAFT

Every player can draft five or more celebrities each year.

ORDER

The player with least points drafts first, then second least, etc.

The player with most points drafts last, then again and it goes back down the line.

Drafting continues until everyone has 50 celebrities on their list.

7. CORONATION OF DIAMONDS

Coronation of Diamonds is the final stage.

If one of your Diamonds died last year, choose a new Diamond from your list.

  • If it’s your new Blue Diamond, they move to the #1 spot on your list.

  • If it’s your new Brown Diamond, they move to the #50 spot on your list.
