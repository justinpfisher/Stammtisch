03 / The tradition

A life remembered.
A story shared.

At the next Stammtisch following a passing, the member presents a Celebration of Life speech about that person’s life and contribution to the world.

How the points work

100−age at passing=points
  • A passing at age 100 earns 10 points.
  • At age 101 or older, 100 minus age gives a penalty: −1 at 101, −2 at 102, and so on.
  • The first and last selections carry double points in the spreadsheet.

“Awarded” points contribute to the standings. For other selections, “Value” shows the points at the age shown. Ages and values use the register’s update date.

Notes on scoring +

Recorded awards and agreed exceptions take precedence over estimates. Kevin Keegan’s 25 points were apportioned 12 to Matt and 13 to Ken. Dolly Parton’s 20 points were already included in Jamie’s total.

Where an award has not yet been recorded, the age-at-death rules determine the provisional figure.

04 / The pool

Official rules.

The Club's agreed rules
Clarifications recorded 21 October 2026

The company has settled these particulars. Earlier scores stand as recorded; the scorekeeper does not guess at a missing death date, a private Protection or the first word in the group chat.

Each member keeps a list of up to 50 celebrities. Their passings earn points, distinctions and, occasionally, the right to interfere with another gentleman's list. The highest score wins the competition year.

POINTS+

A passing is worth 100 minus the celebrity's age at death. A passing at exactly 100 earns 10 points; a passing over 100 incurs the corresponding negative points. The Blue and Brown Diamonds carry double points or double penalties.

Reaching 100 earns no additional Steal or extra Diamond privilege merely by reaching that age. The ordinary Blue and Brown Diamond benefits remain. Previously awarded points and benefits are not rewritten.

DIAMONDS & PROTECTIONS+

The Blue Diamond is selection #1. Its death earns double points and one Steal at the next draft. The Brown Diamond is selection #50. Its death earns double points and one Protection (Blood Diamond) at the next draft.

The member with the youngest celebrity death of the competition year earns a Protection, with age compared to the day when necessary. A Protection may be placed on an eligible celebrity to prevent Steals and Jettisons. It does not double points.

The living Blue and Brown Diamond picks (positions #1 and #50) are protected. Once a separately earned Blood Diamond Protection is placed on an eligible pick, it stays with that celebrity until death, even across competition years. It cannot be cancelled, moved or jettisoned. An earned but unplaced Protection expires at the applicable draft.

Blue and Brown Diamonds cannot be stolen or jettisoned. If either distinction becomes vacant through death, a replacement may be appointed in the final stage of the next draft.

THE YEAR & THE PRIZE+

The competition changes years at 8 p.m. Toronto time on the third Friday of January, the first regular Stammtisch of the year. The next handover is Friday, January 15, 2027, at 8 p.m. From January 1 until that hour, qualifying deaths still count for the previous competition year. The existing selections remain in place until the draft.

The highest scorer receives a $100 gift, chosen and paid for by the other five. Tied winners share the prize.

The Committee will observe the appointed hour. A premature change of year is not a recognized sporting achievement.

BIRTHDAY BUFFET+

When a celebrity's death is first reported in the Club's WhatsApp group on a member's birthday, the birthday gentleman receives the points, regardless of whose list holds the name. The date in the shared CoL spreadsheet is the Club's default first-report date unless a member supplies a correction. The actual death date, private discovery or news alert does not determine the Birthday Buffet. Members remain honour-bound to report promptly.

Blue Diamonds, Brown Diamonds and celebrities bearing a placed Protection are excluded. A death at exactly 100 earns the birthday gentleman 10 points, but no Steal. Existing expressly agreed allocations remain as recorded.

THE ANNUAL ORDER OF BUSINESS+

The annual proceedings have seven stages. Devil's Share follows the earned Steals, by deliberate design.

  1. Declare the winner of the completed competition year, including any shared prize.
  2. Place earned Protections, including those arising from a Brown Diamond or the youngest celebrity death.
  3. Exercise earned Steals in order of the actual deaths that earned them. A Blue Diamond death earns a Steal; the member with the oldest celebrity death also earns one. For the oldest-death Steal, its place in line is determined by the celebrity's actual date of death, not the later date on which the award is declared. A stolen celebrity cannot be stolen again during the same draft.
  4. Devil's Share. The member with the lowest score chooses a Steal or a Protection. Everyone tied for last receives their own choice. This consolation comes after the earned Steals.
  5. Optional Jettisons. Members with fewer than five vacancies may jettison eligible names to make up to five spaces, or decline. Diamonds, placed Protections and celebrities over 100 cannot be jettisoned. If a 100-year-old is jettisoned and dies before 101, the ten-point penalty applies. Jettisoned celebrities return to the available pool.
  6. The snake draft. Fill the actual vacancies, without exceeding 50 names per member. Lowest score chooses first and the highest chooses last, then the order reverses. A tie in draft position is decided by rock, paper, scissors. There is no requirement to manufacture five vacancies or take five new names.
  7. Appoint replacement Diamonds. Vacant Blue or Brown Diamond positions may be filled by new or retained eligible selections. A placed Protection belongs to its celebrity; it is not inherited by a replacement.

An unused earned Steal or an unplaced Protection expires when its applicable draft concludes. A Protection already placed on a living celebrity does not expire with the draft, the season or the Committee's patience. The Club's permanent register records Protection placements and draft actions.

AMENDING THE RULES+

Any one of the six members may send a clear CoL amendment to the Club's Annals address. No second vote, special wording or ceremonial countersignature is needed. The Committee interprets the instruction, updates the website and keeps the former wording on record. If its interpretation has wandered from the Club's intent, the members are invited to correct the record.

A question, joke or recollection is not an instruction to change the rules. Earlier awards remain as recorded unless the Club expressly decides otherwise.

These are the Club's agreed rules. Current standings show the points actually recorded in the authoritative register; no historic points, protection claims or WhatsApp reporting dates have been invented to bring them into line. Earlier signed editions and revisions remain available for the permanent record.
