03 / The tradition

A life remembered.
A story shared.

At the next Stammtisch following a passing, the member presents a Celebration of Life speech about that person’s life and contribution to the world.

How the points work

100−age at passing=points
  • A passing at age 100 earns 10 points.
  • At age 101 or older, 100 minus age gives a penalty: −1 at 101, −2 at 102, and so on.
  • The first and last selections carry double points in the spreadsheet.

“Awarded” points contribute to the standings. For other selections, “Value” shows the points at the age shown. Ages and values use the register’s update date.

04 / The pool

Official rules.

Celebration of Life Pool
Supplied September 20, 2026

Each member of Stammtisch has a list of 50 Celebrities. When a celebrity on your list dies, you get points. Whoever has the most points at the end of the year wins.

POINTS+

The number of points is equal to 100 minus their age at time of death.

If the celebrity is 100 years old when they die, you receive 10 points, and a Steal at the next draft.

If the celebrity is over 100 when they die, you lose points. The number of points is equal to their age minus 100.

BLUE DIAMOND+

The first Celebrity on your list, #1, is your Blue Diamond.

When your Blue Diamond dies, you get double points (or lose double points, if they’re over 100.) You also earn a Steal at the next draft.

BROWN DIAMOND+

The last Celebrity on your list, #50, is your Brown Diamond.

When your Blue Diamond dies, you get double points (or lose double points, if they’re over 100.) You also earn a Blood Diamond at the next draft. (Blood Diamond is a protection you place on one of your celebrities, making them unstealable.)

Wording note: The supplied paragraph says “Blue Diamond.” The Brown Diamond heading and draft instructions refer to the Brown Diamond; this wording is awaiting clarification.

PRIZE+

The person with the highest score for the year (January 1 to December 31) gets a $100 gift, paid for and chosen by the other five.

BIRTHDAY BUFFET+

If we discover a celebrity dies on someone’s birthday, the birthday boy gets the points, regardless of whose list their on. (NOTE: Birthday Buffet points are based on when we learn the death occurred, not necessarily the date of death. A player cannot withhold information of a death to benefit.)

If a 100-year-old dies, they get the 10 points, but NOT the Steal.

Diamonds are excluded from Birthday Buffet.

DRAFTING+

Every January, we draft new Celebrities until we each have 50 again.

DRAFT - ORDER OF BUSINESS+

FIRST - Blood Diamonds

If your Brown Diamond died in the previous calendar year, you add a Blood Diamond to someone on your list, making them ineligible for Steals and Jettisons. (Note Blood Diamonds do NOT give you double-points when they die, unlike the Blue or Brown Diamond.)

PROPOSED NEW RULE: Whoever had the youngest celebrity death the previous year also gets a Blood Diamond.

SECOND - Steals

Steals go next. (Remember, you’re eligible for a steal if you had a Celebrity die at 100, or if your Blue Diamond died.) Steals happen in the same order as the deaths prompting them.

You can steal any Celebrity from anyone else’s list, excluding Diamonds. Once a Celebrity is stolen, they can’t be stolen again until next year.

THIRD - Devil’s Share

The player with the lowest points from the previous year gets either one Steal OR one Blood Diamond.

THIRD - Jettisons

If you have fewer than five spots available on your list, you can jettison Celebrities until you have five. You can waive this right if you prefer to keep your current Celebrities. (In that case you won’t get five new picks.) If you have five or more spots available, you’re not eligible to jettison.

You can’t jettison a Diamond, or a Celebrity over 100 years.

You can jettison a 100-year-old Celebrity, but if they die before they turn 101, you lose ten points.

Once all players have five spots available, or have chosen not to jettison, jettisoning is complete.

All jettisoned Celebrities become immediately available for the draft.

FOURTH - Draft

Your new drafts replace empty slots on your list.

The person with lowest score for the previous year drafts first, followed by second lowest, etc. The person with the highest overall score goes last, then drafts again and it goes back down the line.

Drafting continues until all lists are back to 50 celebrities.

FIFTH - New Diamonds

If any of your Diamonds died the previous year, they can be replaced by any of your new drafts, or by moving an existing celebrity from your list into the Diamond spot. (Note that when a Blood Diamond dies, the Blood Diamond is lost… Unlike a Blue Diamond or Brown Diamond, the distinction dies with the celebrity.)

The youngest-celebrity-death rule remains a proposal. The draft order retains the supplied labels, including the two “THIRD” steps.
