# Club History narrative staging

The [Club History section of the Brand and Editorial Style Guide](brand-editorial-style-guide.md#club-history) governs both the opening and later chapters. Compose chronological paragraphs in the third-person institutional voice of the Club, the company, or the record. Use names and aliases only in protected evidence. A chapter can carry one quiet editorial observation when suitable. Treat poignant material sincerely.

Keep the exact retained source, signed Annals receipt, active rights and any conflicting accounts in private evidence. The existing one-shot assessment is the only proposed generation route. It can propose new prose and a clause-to-claim plan. The proposal needs an independent, source-grounded review of every factual clause and of the exact copy before it can be qualified. That authority supplies a reviewed digest binding the current edition, the proposal, and the verified claim evidence. A model-supplied digest or evidence label cannot qualify a proposal. Review the actual text for unsupported dates, invented proceedings, motives, or consensus, and verify that the prose preserves uncertainty and the voices of differing accounts.

Identify excluded origin evidence before presenting an eligible fact set. Keep the excluded trip and unadopted bylaws out of both opening and later claims, even when they appear in old signed editions. Historical signatures remain evidence of what was published at the time. The next opening uses its separately approved evidence and copy. Fixtures use invented exhibit material only.

The offline `HistoryNarrative.mjs` staging contract checks the reviewed binding, chapter scope, chronology, name and first-person exclusions, excerpt duplication, competing accounts and observation limits. A disputed fact needs both the source bindings and reviewed distinguishing wording for every eligible version. The contract projects only approved public source references and applies portrait permission per use. A failure returns the exact previous chapters. A success returns candidate chapters for staging, with neither a signed revision nor a dispatch. A roster has its own permission and display decision outside narrative provenance and contributor credits.

Production integration remains a separate review on this draft. Establish the trusted semantic-review input and remove the earlier excerpt format from the automatic addition path before enabling composed History. Reuse the current consent, receipt, recovery and signed publication controls for the approved output. Keep source assertions and identity crosswalks private.
