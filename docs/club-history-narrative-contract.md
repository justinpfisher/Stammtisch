# Club History narrative staging

The [Club History section of the Brand and Editorial Style Guide](brand-editorial-style-guide.md#club-history) governs the opening and later chapters. Compose chronological paragraphs in the third-person institutional voice of the Club, the company, or the record. Keep personal names and aliases in protected evidence. Treat poignant material sincerely. The Style Guide allows one quiet observation per section. A routine insertion leaves observations out because older signed sections do not identify their existing observations.

The existing one-shot Annals assessment can propose one composed insertion using exact spans from the member's retained source. It uses the same request and budget reservation as the Annals assessment. Before classification, one separate reference message can carry a recent public sentence or short paragraph for each available chapter, the canonical Club-authored example from the Style Guide, and the existing solemn example. The reference is bounded to 2,000 characters and contains chapter headings and safe prose. Only the submitted source can supply facts and wording for the new account. The final input message remains the sole source for claims and the existing recipe, quotation, and photo assessment.

The intake batch reads its optional reference once from the last privately reconciled public edition and its matching verification record. This adds no public network reads to intake. The private response record signs the authenticated contribution ID, immutable source fingerprint, exact sent request and retained attempt, selected chapter and revision, and original response fingerprint. The existing private receipt signer uses a separate domain for this proof. It cannot authorize an entry or a public dispatch. Preview checks that binding, the approved tone examples and the selected chapter. A missing binding or changed chapter holds the History prose while ordinary Annals drafting continues. An unrelated chapter can advance before a fresh preview. The existing main and live checks still run before signing. An assessment is never repeated to fill missing context.

Once the Annals version is signed and public, the owner desk can preview the candidate beside its private claim evidence. To review it, open the source item, load the current History, select the insertion point, and preview the composed draft. Check every factual clause and its claims against the retained source and signed Annals copy, including dates, chronology, uncertainty, competing accounts, narrative voice, and whether it belongs at that point in the chapter. Confirm the exact preview only if it passes.

The existing signer rechecks the previous edition, proposed chapter, exact source and assessment, signed Annals version, and current permissions before dispatch. An insertion preserves other passages and chapters. A changed input, missing consent, uncertain dispatch, or unsupported source holds the change and leaves the last good edition in place. The intake trigger can reconcile and remove revoked material and credits. It does not add prose from old excerpt drafts or publish model proposals. Private claims and the identity crosswalk stay in the owner desk and private release evidence. Only used public sources contribute a portrait credit when permission is active. The Club roster is a separate display decision.

A separately approved Annals correction can automatically update a composed passage's source digest when the correction adds the first approved photo to the same cocktail entry. The entire prior entry must match the existing digest after removing only that photo. The prose, chapter position, and source link stay as published. A text, recipe, date, or other content change follows the existing withdrawal-of-support rules. The private intake then verifies the committed and served revision and reconciles its ledger without dispatching again.

A History-only withdrawal remains attached to the contribution through later Annals corrections. Separate contributions keep their usual eligibility. When several signed Annals withdrawals overtake an accepted History dispatch, the intake verifies the original committed edition and each successor against its immediate parent's archive. It checks the relevant signed entry and approval records and matches each retirement to its existing private signed withdrawal and accepted dispatch. It also checks the final edition against the live pages before reconciling.

The binding and structural checks are mechanical. A span matching the source and signed Annals is evidence of those words. The model's mapping cannot establish that its paraphrase follows from those words, that competing accounts are complete, or that an unfamiliar term is not a member alias. The existing owner confirmation supplies the independent editorial judgment on the exact candidate. Keep the excluded origin trip and unadopted bylaws out of both opening and later accounts. Previously signed editions and original records remain intact. Tests use fictional material.
