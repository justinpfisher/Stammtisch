## Operating contract

### Complete-email interpretation

The subject and body are separate, equally valid source fields. Either may be empty; the AI receives both with field labels and proposes the category, intent (direct quotation, reported speech, anecdote, recipe, description or other), speaker identity and exact evidence field. For a quotation it also proposes the exact words. These fields are editorial proposals only. Deterministic code requires the proposed words to match the indicated original subject/body and the speaker to be supported by an explicit source label or a clear cross-field pairing. It accepts the six approved identities, including Justin/Fish, and does not derive a speaker from the email sender. A mismatched name, ambiguous mention, unknown speaker, non-verbatim extraction, non-quotation intent or conflict between fields stays private. Reported speech and anecdotes do not become quotations merely because they mention somebody speaking.

The same subject/body rule applies to recipes, club observations and other categories: a subject such as “Cocktail from Friday” can provide context for a recipe in the body; a complete recipe or non-sensitive observation can also stand alone in either field. Original recipe quantities, units, ingredients, syrup preparation and authorship remain source-grounded. The receipt date is not evidence of an event date or attendance. Messages with no usable text or supported attachment are held. Any clipped or over-limit field is explicitly marked incomplete and cannot be drafted or published; long subject-only quotations are never silently shortened.

Previously held items are not automatically retried. The owner-only desk may offer a one-time deterministic reassessment only for an existing saved quotation draft and its exact original source. It requires the owner's explicit confirmation, matching private source/draft digests, active receipt-time and current permissions, and proof that no approval or dispatch was attempted. It makes no AI request, preserves the original hold and writes a separate recovery record. An uncertain recovery or dispatch is stopped without retry.

The owner-only desk distinguishes receipt/authentication, private draft, eligibility, a specific hold reason, dispatch awaiting live verification, verified live publication and failure/recovery states. No public submission, AI output or consent evidence is shown outside the private desk.

Verify exactly six distinct mailboxes privately; never put the list, authentication records, raw mail or user contacts in public GitHub files, issues, PRs, Actions logs or builds. A `From:` match is insufficient: preserve the existing single Google `Authentication-Results` aligned DKIM/DMARC validation; hold ambiguous or unverifiable forwarded mail.

An owner attestation is recorded separately from authenticated replies and cannot be forged into one. The invitation plainly explains public GitHub history, potential persistence after withdrawal, conspicuous censorship, anonymous credit, ownership and third-party limitations. An owner invitation is **not** a member’s consent. Members may revoke either permission by authenticated exact-command email or the owner-only desk. Revocation prevents new automatic publication; it does not erase existing public history.
3. The system records consent privately at receipt and checks both live consents again before publication. It cannot retrospectively publish emails received before affirmative publication consent. The AI model categorises and extracts details into a **private draft**; it has no tools, cannot grant permission, and is constrained to a pinned model, structured output and the existing application budget.
4. `AutoCore.js` is a narrow deterministic eligibility filter. It publishes only a verified, allowed category unless a **material** unresolved AI flag concerns privacy, ownership, speaker permission, accurate recipe transcription or security. Generic editorial uncertainty does not block publication, and an unsupported AI headline falls back to the actual subject. Self-authored quotations introduced explicitly or clearly paired with the sender may publish anonymously under the contributor's standing consent; a speaker portrait requires that speaker's distinct active quotation-and-portrait permission. Quotes by another member require that person's permission predating receipt and still active, or remain held. The exact quoted words must be identifiable in the original subject or body and must match the AI extraction character for character after removing only structural speaker labels and surrounding quote delimiters. The public summary uses the contributor's actual wording. For cocktail recipes, recorded ingredient and method lines retain the author's actual words and original measurements. Missing instructions or amounts can be supplemented with **clearly marked suggested preparation / quantities** in a separate editorial section. Those approximations are neither historical evidence nor replacements for recorded syrup quantities; ambiguous source measurements are not silently presented as verified. Unrelated banter and editorial commentary need not appear inside the recipe. Text stays publicly anonymous unless manual credit is approved, or a separate previously recorded member permission allows the small illustrated portrait of the authenticated contributor to appear without a visible name or email. Quotation cards instead use the speaker's portrait, even if someone else submitted the email. The public `Submitted YYYY-MM` label is the receipt period, **not a fabricated event date**. No AI paraphrase or unconfirmed date is silently presented as a club fact.
5. Strong profanity receives visible `[EXPLETIVE]` substitutions; `[POOP]` is used for the occasional taste/smell expression. Mild words such as “damn” and “hell” remain. Originals stay private. The public renderer refuses unredacted strong profanity, including on the manual path. Any censored direct quotation must visibly say it is **not verbatim**, retain the uncensored source privately, and have the actual speaker’s public-use permission. Self-authored quotations with the member’s standing consent can publish; other-person quotations without their permission, identifying anecdotes, allegations, uncertain historical attribution, ambiguous recipes and other material unresolved concerns are **held privately**, not rewritten into a different story.
6. Suitable photographs **can publish automatically** after the contributor's scoped standing permission and a positive private AI image-safety check. The current bounded first release supports one JPEG/PNG/WEBP/HEIC/HEIF source image per submission if the existing Drive service can provide a small baseline JPEG thumbnail; it strips image metadata, checks dimensions/structure and signs **only that derivative** (up to 32 KiB) for GitHub. A small, isolated non-identifying cocktail or club object is eligible; identifying people, guests, interiors, reflections, readable private details, failed conversion, unsupported PDFs, multiple images and uncertain rights are held privately for exception handling. The original stays private. A safe independent text entry may still publish if the photo is held. The registered owner attestation does not make AI identification or photo screening infallible. Google Drive's thumbnail method can return null; no alternative paid image processor or public-original fallback is allowed.
7. Only the exact eligible entry is signed in private. Automatic entries use the distinct `annals-auto-entry` repository dispatch with separate domain-tagged `standing-consent-text-v1`, `standing-consent-image-v1`, and restricted speaker-specific `standing-consent-quote-v1` HMAC modes. The image mode binds the exact small metadata-stripped JPEG digest, alternative text and separate contributor photo permission to the signed entry. GitHub checks the signature, exact public fields, category, no-photo rule, replay and duplicate protection before committing to `main`. The separate manual signature path, owner-only corrections and withdrawals remain. No private evidence or source message crosses into public GitHub. The site creates only populated categories; no pending or empty category appears.
8. Dispatch HTTP 204 means only **accepted for processing**, never “published”. The private reconciliation step must observe the matching signed entry/digest in the public archive **and** its entry ID on live GitHub Pages before recording `verified_live` and attempting a one-time contributor notification. Failed/unknown deliveries, interrupted provider attempts, consent ambiguity and failed publication are held. **Never automatically re-dispatch an uncertain attempt.** If a signed dispatch is still unverified after 24 hours, the private owner receives one status notice; this is not a claim of public success and does not cause a duplicate dispatch.
9. Submissions about celebrity deaths must never change Celebration of Life scores, register, death verification or OneSignal alerts. No new app, account, paid subscription, runner or public self-hosting is required.

## Owner-identified test quarantine

The private desk can permanently quarantine an exact submission hash. Its write-once private record preserves the original, consent, AI attempt, ledger and audit evidence. Intake holds a quarantined hash even before it has been staged; automatic drafting, publication, manual dispatch and held-quote reassessment refuse it. No automatic release exists. Existing approval or dispatch markers require inspection and, where applicable, the authenticated withdrawal workflow before quarantine. Do not classify real submissions as tests or copy private item identifiers or reasons into this public repository.


### Private AI failure evidence

Each new one-shot HTTP-200 provider response is retained, before validation, in owner-only Drive as ai-response-private.json, bounded to 100,000 characters. This unapproved output must never appear in execution logs, GitHub, builds or public previews. Invalid, oversized or unavailable response evidence remains held; the attempt and budget reservation are never cleared, refunded or retried. Earlier failures without retained response evidence cannot be reconstructed from a generic invalid_response marker. Inspect the private evidence before deciding a correction or a separately authored new diagnostic is necessary.


### Text-only no-image response repair

For a request proven to have no image input, a provider none/true image-safety flag is conservatively canonicalised to none/false. Requests with images or unknown input context retain strict image validation. The original response is preserved. An owner-authenticated one-time repair can reconstruct an eligible private draft from an already completed captured response containing only this diagnosed flag inconsistency. It requires unchanged exact source text, active receipt-time and current consent, no images, no quarantine or prior approval/dispatch, and the normal deterministic source-grounding policy.
