# Annals publication and privacy

Current owner decisions reconciled 18 October 2026. This governs content and consent; [current state](current-state.md) records which capabilities are enabled, and the [runbook](annals-production-runbook.md) governs operation. The 8 October email proposal and early per-item approval plan are historical.

## Default editorial approach

Publish eligible, authentic member contributions automatically under recorded scoped standing permissions. No routine per-item owner review or repeated consent is required where existing evidence covers the use. An allowlisted sender or a submitted email alone is not consent. Preserve the provenance of owner-attested permissions as owner attestation; never fabricate member replies, backdate consent or revive a revocation.

**Default-post rule:** Once a sender is authenticated and their recorded standing permissions cover the use, a source-grounded Annals entry publishes without another owner approval. The source remains private. Prefer a faithful screened public derivative. **All six members' first names—Justin, Marc, Matt, Ken, Jamie and Jerome—and their established approved club nicknames may appear naturally and unchanged** in otherwise eligible recipes, recollections, monthly reports and keepsakes. The Club owner has explicitly attested all six members' approval for these ordinary references; Fish is Justin's verified nickname. Do not invent a nickname, use an unverified alias to infer the identity of a quotation speaker, or insert a name that the original source did not supply. No `[MEMBER]` replacement or separate per-mention approval is required. This narrow permission does not override an individual's recorded revocation or separate quotation, depicted-photo, privacy or third-party rights. Harmless quotation marks are not themselves proof of third-party quotation. Actual reported speech, attributed Register of Remarks quotations, substantive private details, guests and other people's rights still require their own distinct evidence or omission.

**Flagged-content rule — publish the safe remainder:** A flag asks the editorial process to remove only what actually crosses a rights, safety or accuracy boundary; it is not itself a rejection. Prefer a narrowly masked contact detail (`[DETAIL OMITTED]`), omit a separable sensitive sentence or third-party quotation when permission is missing, and publish the independently meaningful remainder. A held or unsuitable image must not suppress independent, safe text. The complete original remains owner-only. For ordinary Annals, prepare the AI draft from the screened derivative, not from unredacted contact details or confidential passages. Keep potentially publishable first names and established nicknames unchanged. If the remaining text would misrepresent an event, conceal a material recipe measurement/step, distort an exact quote or remove the substance of the post, hold that component and notify the owner and authenticated contributor rather than inventing a replacement. CoL instructions may not be stripped of their governance meaning and republished as ordinary Annals; they retain independent release safeguards.

Ordinary member-submitted historical recollections, monthly proceedings and artefacts may appear in the public Annals, labelled by the verified **submission period**, without silently altering the separate canonical Club History chapter ledger. A formal chapter-fact revision still follows its existing supported-evidence and correction workflow. CoL rule changes likewise stay on their independently qualified route.

The Annals records actual gatherings, Assemblies, recipes, remarks and keepsakes. Do not invent history, attendance, dates, quotations, creators, rules or decisions. Minor wording, heading or category uncertainty is not a hold; concrete privacy, rights, security or material-accuracy problems are. The four surviving collections and Photos/History links appear only after genuine qualifying content exists. No example club memories.

Everything in the website and public GitHub, including branches, previews, PRs, logs and old revisions, is public. Keep raw mail, private conversations, originals, contacts, consent evidence, private logistics and reference photographs in approved private storage. Public removal cannot erase all Git history or external caches.

## Publication boundaries

| Material | Operative rule |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary source-grounded text | Authenticated six-member source; active private AI and standing-publication permission at receipt and again before publication; anonymous by default |
| Recipes/handwriting | Preserve original quantities, units, ingredients and syrup steps. Hold materially ambiguous transcription. Missing amounts or steps may receive separately labelled editorial suggestions, never claimed as the original |
| Exact quotations | Match the original subject/body. A speaker's illustrated attribution requires the distinct active speaker-specific quote/likeness grant; do not infer the speaker from the submitter. Unpermitted third-party quotations stay private |
| Contributor illustration | Actual submitter's distinct revocable portrait grant, separate from speaker attribution. Reuse the six approved derivatives. A quotation represents its speaker |
| Ordinary photos of the six members, drinks, objects or scenery | Owner's privately recorded standing attestation supersedes per-image approval within scope. Preserve each depiction/image revocation and positive safety screening. Member-only source evidence and all six depiction grants are required for group images; AI does not authenticate faces |
| Guests, children, unknown people, sensitive allegations, uncertain ownership, identifying private locations/interiors or readable private screens/documents | Hold privately for specific permission/clarification or omission; a warning label does not cure missing rights or unsafe disclosure |

**Installed baseline (owner-only Version 53): JPEG only.** The guarded source supports one to three JPEG inputs, at most 8 MiB each, with bounded original-pixel orientation/resize/re-encode, metadata removal and SHA-bound signing. A separately reviewed common-format expansion, once actually installed in the same private project, accepts **still PNG, WebP, HEIC and HEIF** as input where Google Drive provides a sufficiently large private JPEG preview. The preview must pass bounded pixel decode/resize/re-encode and the same independent image-safety/consent checks; **public assets remain metadata-free JPEG only**. Animated formats, multi-frame sequences, mixed unsupported attachments, spoofed MIME, undersized/invalid or unavailable previews and unsupported formats stay private. The Google Drive preview API does not guarantee a preview for every HEIC/HEIF image or a particular resolution. Safe independent text may proceed without a held image; a real accepted photograph is not proven by MIME recognition alone. The previously retired legacy unscreened thumbnail-publication fallback stays retired. The effective installed version and exact remaining qualification are maintained in [current state](current-state.md), not inferred from this source policy.

Existing private permission records may support future ordinary six-member photographs without another image-by-image owner action. New people, new sensitive uses and unclear photographer/recipe rights require actual authority. Keep originals private; never publish the reference collection or assume an automatic crop removes every risk.

**Cocktail editorial presentation (owner direction, 19 October 2026):** A publicly screened and signed photograph of a drink may additionally receive a **clearly identified, derived Moody & Atmospheric display treatment** generated from the approved JPEG by the existing hosted publisher. The separate editorial asset is not a claim about the original room, lighting or occasion. The signed source JPEG, its provenance, alt text, recipe and correction history remain unchanged and directly accessible; the decorative rendition receives neither fabricated people nor new recipe facts. Any unclear or unsafe segmentation falls back to the original, without holding eligible content or permitting a second paid AI attempt. Reuse existing photo rights within their recorded scope; this addition does not authorize editing sensitive imagery, changing a depiction grant, exposing private source photographs or silently substituting an editorial image for its documentary original. Remove current editorial derivatives upon the existing authorized photo withdrawal, recognizing that public Git history may persist.

## Voice, quotations and corrections

Use dry, witty mock-institutional ceremony. Maintain exact quotations except the approved conspicuous euphemisms: strong profanity becomes **[EXPLETIVE]**, with contextual **[POOP]** where appropriate; mild “damn” and “hell” remain. Censored quotations are labelled **not verbatim**. Original words remain privately preserved.

**The Register of Remarks** pairs a verified quotation with its actual speaker's approved illustration, subject to the separate permission above. No invented sayings. Submitted month/year is the receipt period, not an invented event date.

The permanent footer reads:

> The Editorial Committee reserves the right to replace particularly spirited language with conspicuous euphemisms. Historical accuracy is otherwise maintained. The Editorial Committee is actually just AI we tried to train.

**Submission status:** An acknowledged email is not yet a published entry. An actual deterministic hold or interrupted draft receives a one-shot private exception notice to the owner and a generic non-publication notice to the authenticated contributor; a pre-draft processing hold is reported if it persists for at least an hour. The owner receives a private item link, not source text in public logs. A contributor receives the final live link only after publication is independently verified. Accepted dispatches unverified for 24 hours generate a separate owner alert. Email delivery can itself fail; private journals and the owner desk retain the status. No repeated provider or signed dispatch attempt is authorised by a reminder.

Corrections and withdrawals use the existing signed paths and live reconciliation. Preserve private originals, prior signed records and replay protection. Revocation stops future use; it does not prove historical copies disappeared. Do not automatically retry uncertain publication.

Annals submissions never directly change CoL scores or trigger death notifications. Authenticated prospective CoL requests use the separate qualified rule workflow; questions, jokes and recollections are not adopted rules.
