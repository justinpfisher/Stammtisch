On Matters Worth Committing to Posterity +

Contributions are invited to the collected works of six gentlemen. Literary merit is welcome; relevance to the company will suffice.

  • Cocktails & recipes: For the advancement of the liquid arts. Include ingredients, quantities, and instructions—including any homemade syrups. A photo is welcome, should the spirit move you.
  • The Register of Remarks: A turn of phrase that deserves another turn. Send the exact words, who said them, and any essential context. Wit may be borrowed; attribution must be correct.
  • Club History & keepsakes: Every institution has its chapters. Send a recollection, the origins of a tradition, or an artefact worth preserving. We’ll weave members’ contributions into a history, piece by piece. Fragments welcome; a magnum opus is unnecessary.
  • Celebration of Life rules: Submit a revision or addition, stating the change and when it takes effect. Please supply the clause, not merely the cause for complaint.

Address the record

Email annals@stammtischbrewery.com from your recognised member address. Write normally and attach what you have. No forms, categories, or command of the subjunctive required.

Uncertain dates and details should be identified as such. Memory may take poetic licence; the record should acknowledge it.

Discretion remains the better part of publication. The Annals are public. Keep private addresses, confidential matters, and other people’s material out unless permission is in place.

Corrections are welcome at the same address. The last word need not be the final version.

Kingston Windfall cocktail recipe

Submitted October 2026

The Kingston Windfall is a fall-inspired craft cocktail that combines Jamaican rum with autumnal apple cider and spice notes.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) Jamaican rum
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) Freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) Apple cider syrup (or cinnamon apple cider syrup)
  • ¼ oz (7.5 ml) Honey syrup (3:1 ratio of honey to water)
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Preparation

  • Combine all ingredients into a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
  • Shake vigorously until well-chilled.
  • Strain the mixture into a chilled coupe glass.
  • Garnish with a dash of bitters on top and a light dusting of cinnamon.
