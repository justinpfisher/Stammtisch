On Matters Worth Committing to Posterity +

Contributions from the six authenticated Club members are invited to the collected works of six gentlemen. Literary merit is welcome; relevance to the company will suffice.

  • Cocktails & recipes: For the advancement of the liquid arts. Include ingredients, quantities, and instructions—including any homemade syrups. A photo is welcome, should the spirit move you.
  • The Register of Remarks: A turn of phrase that deserves another turn. Send the exact words, who said them, and any essential context. Quotation publication requires the actual speaker and applicable rights to be supported.
  • Club History & keepsakes: The past remains less orderly than the official record would prefer. Send a recollection, the origins of a tradition, or an artefact worth preserving. The Editorial Committee will shape supported contributions into one continuing account; fragments are entirely acceptable.
  • The Register of Miscellany: For stray observations, small curiosities, and other bits of Stammtisch life that don’t fit anywhere else but deserve to be annalled.
  • Celebration of Life rules: Rules and amendments are maintained exclusively on the Celebration of Life page; authenticated members may propose changes by email.

Address the record

Email annals@stammtischbrewery.com from your authenticated Club member address with a story, recipe, quotation or correction. Write normally and attach what you have. Eligible text may be published anonymously after screening. Official Club decisions remain members’ business. No forms, categories, or command of the subjunctive required.

Uncertain dates and details should be identified as such. Memory may take poetic licence; the record should acknowledge it.

Discretion remains the better part of publication. The Annals are public. Keep private addresses, confidential matters, and other people’s material out unless permission is in place.

Corrections are welcome at the same address. The last word need not be the final version.

The Cocktail Register

Daq in Black (Amaro Nonino Edition)

Submitted November 20, 2026 (Toronto) · Event date unrecorded

↗

Ingredients

  • 1 oz dark Jamaican rum (such as Blackwell, Appleton Estate 8 Year, or Coruba)
  • 0.5 oz Cognac (VS or VSOP)
  • 0.5 oz Amaro Nonino
  • 0.5 oz demerara syrup (1:1 or rich 2:1, depending on how sweet you prefer)
  • 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 dash Fee Brothers Black Walnut Bitters

Preparation

  • Garnish. Expressed lemon peel.
  • Chill the glassware. Fill your coupe with ice water or place it in the freezer before you start measuring.
  • Combine ingredients. Add the Jamaican rum, Cognac, Amaro Nonino, demerara syrup, lime juice, and black walnut bitters into a cocktail shaker.
  • Shake vigorously. Fill the shaker two-thirds full with solid ice cubes and shake hard for 10 to 12 seconds until the outside of the tin is frosty.
  • Double-strain. Dump the ice from your coupe and strain the cocktail through a fine-mesh strainer to catch any small ice shards.
  • Garnish and serve. Express a fresh strip of lemon peel over the surface of the drink to release the oils, rub the rim lightly, and drop it in.

The Cocktail Register

Kingston Windfall cocktail recipe

Submitted October 2026

↗

The Kingston Windfall is a fall-inspired craft cocktail that combines Jamaican rum with autumnal apple cider and spice notes.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz (45 ml) Jamaican rum
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) Freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • ¾ oz (22 ml) Apple cider syrup (or cinnamon apple cider syrup)
  • ¼ oz (7.5 ml) Honey syrup (3:1 ratio of honey to water)
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Preparation

  • Combine all ingredients into a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
  • Shake vigorously until well-chilled.
  • Strain the mixture into a chilled coupe glass.
  • Garnish with a dash of bitters on top and a light dusting of cinnamon.
