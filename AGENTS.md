## Execution

- An authorised change includes implementation, relevant tests, review/merge, Pages deployment and verification of the affected live result. For wording or layout, use focused checks; for functionality, affected regression/browser checks; for identity, private publication, CoL scoring or payments, add only relevant integrity, rights, replay and source checks. Do not rerun unrelated acceptance, consent registration, account activation, full-source reconciliation or key rotation.
- Normal path: member decision → versioned GitHub change → proportional checks → automatic GitHub Pages publication → affected live readback → completion or one actionable exception. No habitual manual Pages builds. Target about 1–3 minutes for Chat changes and a few minutes for member email; measure rather than promise.
- Corrections, withdrawals and historical edits require attribution and preservation of non-sensitive history, **never a new-death push**. Only a newly independently confirmed passing may trigger a deduplicated death notification.
- Preserve approved, screened facts, recipe measurements, original images, approved portraits, genuine quotations and scoped speaker/depiction rights. Safe text may publish without optional imagery. Do not fabricate history, private consent, quotations or synthetic documentary photos.
- Annual CoL handover: 8 p.m. America/Toronto on the third Friday of January (next January 15, 2027). If actual death time is unknown on that Friday, assign the **previous** competition year unless a member corrects it. Keep past awards intact until explicitly corrected; preserve permanent placed Protections, the seven-stage annual draft and maximum 50 numbered selections.

Do not claim an atmospheric edit ran or remove rights/source fidelity checks. Duplicate billable attempts remain forbidden.

GMKtec is preferred for interactive development when genuinely accessible. Retain independent source/death verification, narrowly scoped machine credentials and required credential-expiry protection until a verified durable replacement is installed.
