The Stammtisch Social Club

Informality.
With ceremony.

An association dedicated to elevating the longstanding tradition of gathering with intention, consistency, and an admirable degree of ceremony.

Composed of a select membership, the Club maintains a reputation for structure and significance disproportionate to the simplicity of its activities.

Our aim is uncomplicated. Our commitment to process, tradition, and the occasion itself is maintained with a seriousness that has become one of our defining qualities.
